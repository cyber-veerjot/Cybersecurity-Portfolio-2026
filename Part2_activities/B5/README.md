## B5 – Attend Two Cybersecurity Related Talks/Seminars
### Seminar 1 – AI in Defensive Cybersecurity:
I went to a session at the University of Western Australia's CITS2006 Defensive Cybersecurity unit called "AI in Defensive Cybersecurity."
### Topics Covered:
- Artificial intelligence in cybersecurity
- Threat detection using AI
- Machine learning for anomaly detection
- Automated defensive systems
- Real-time monitoring and analysis
### Key Things Learned:
- The identification of internet risks can be speed using AI
- Unusual behaviour in networks and systems can be recognised by machine learning models.
- Analysts' manual labour is lessened by AI-based security solutions.
- Compared to conventional techniques, automated systems can react to threats more quickly.
- AI is becoming more and more crucial to contemporary cybersecurity defense.
### Reflection:
I now have a better understanding of the application of AI technologies in defensive cybersecurity thanks to this lecture. I discovered how machine learning may help with incident response, monitoring, and threat identification in practical settings.
### Seminar 2 – Fraud Detection in the Banking Sector:
I attended a guest lecture by Wellington Muvunduke, a fraud analyst at Bankwest, titled "Fraud Detection in the Banking Sector."
### Topics Covered:
- Financial fraud and banking security
- Account takeover attacks
- Identity theft and card fraud
- SIM swap attacks and phishing scams
- AI and fraud detection systems
- Fraud analyst investigation workflows
### Key Things Learned:
- Human behaviour and social engineering are closely related to fraud.
- AI and behavioural analysis are used by banks to identify questionable transactions.
- Fraud analysts look at unusual login behavior and transaction trends.
- One of the biggest problems in fraud detection is false positives and false negatives.
- Careers in fraud prevention benefit from cybersecurity abilities like anomaly detection and log analysis.
### Reflection:
This session shed light on the application of cybersecurity in the banking industry. I discovered how fraud analysts use AI tools, behavioural analysis, and technical monitoring to identify and stop financial crime.
