## B28 - Produce a cyber safety flyer for (choose 1): elders, high school students, CEOs, Uni students
### Description:
I made a cyber safety awareness flyer specifically for senior citizens as part of this project. It highlighted common threats to online safety as well as useful defensive cybersecurity techniques that can assist senior citizens safeguard their online accounts and personal data.
### Flyer Content:
"Stay Safe Online – Cyber Safety for Elders" is a flyer I made that offers straightforward cybersecurity guidance specifically for senior citizens. The following important safety advice is included in the flyer:
* avoiding scam calls,
* enabling multi-factor authentication (MFA),
* avoiding suspicious links,
* securing Wi-Fi networks,
* using strong passwords,
* and seeking help from trusted individuals when suspicious activity occurs online.
To enhance reading and accessibility for senior populations, the flyer was created with straightforward language, large text, and clear style.
### Analysis:
This exercise illustrated the significance of user education and cybersecurity awareness, especially for older people who are frequently the targets of frauds and social engineering attempts. The flyer aimed to lower the risk of fraud, phishing, and unauthorised account access while encouraging safer online conduct.
