## B9 - Participate in an industry-related cybersecurity event
### Description:
I attended an industry-related cybersecurity event organised by the University Computer Club in partnership with IBM and IBM Z personnel. The seminar concentrated on IBM Z mainframe systems, corporate technology, and cybersecurity-related job prospects in large corporations and critical enterprise settings.
### Topics Covered:
- IBM Z enterprise mainframe systems
- Enterprise computing environments
- Cybersecurity in large-scale systems
- Digital transaction security
- Industry demand for IBM Z skills
- Career pathways in enterprise cybersecurity and infrastructure
### Key Things Learned:
- IBM Z mainframes handle a significant portion of worldwide digital commerce.
- Enterprise systems require robust cybersecurity safeguards because they enable key business operations.
- Mainframe environments prioritize dependability, security, and availability.
- Cybersecurity professionals are required to protect company infrastructure and sensitive data.
- Graduates with expertise in enterprise and mainframe technologies are in high demand in the industry.
### Reflection:
This event helped me better grasp the importance of cybersecurity in enterprise and large-scale computer systems. I learnt how IBM Z systems serve vital digital infrastructure and why enterprise environments require security, reliability, and system availability. The program also gave information about cybersecurity employment options in business systems and infrastructure security.
