## B3 - Discover 3 proactive security implementations in practice.
### Description:
In order to detect and stop cyber attacks before they happen, I discovered three proactive security measures that are employed in real-world settings.
### Identified Proactive Security Implementations:
1. **Security Information and Event Management (SIEM)**
SIEM systems gather and examine logs from various sources, including networks, servers, and applications.
- Why it's proactive: Security teams can take action before a significant problem happens because SIEM solutions can identify odd patterns or suspicious activities in real time.
2. **Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS)**
An intrusion detection system (IDS) scans network traffic for known attack signatures or unusual activity.
- Why it takes initiative: It warns administrators before loss is done by identifying possible threats like malware activity or unauthorised access attempts.
3. **Security Awareness Training**
Employers often train staff members on safe online practices, password security, and phishing.
- Why it's proactive: Educating people helps stop attacks like phishing and social engineering before they happen because many attacks target human behaviour.
### Analysis:
Instead of responding to an attack, these proactive security measures concentrate on early detection and prevention. While security awareness training lessens human-related risks, SIEM and IDS technologies continuously monitor systems to detect threats in real time. When combined, they assist organisations in enhancing overall security resilience and preventing cyber incidents.

This activity includes screenshots and examples of proactive security implementations observed in real-world systems and applications.
