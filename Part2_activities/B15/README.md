## B15 - Teach an elderly person about cybersecurity topic of your choice
### Description:
I used this exercise to teach an older family member about account security and password safety. Password reuse, the dangers of weak passwords, and the significance of employing more robust authentication techniques to enhance online security were the main topics of debate.
### Discussion Summary:
I clarified throughout the conversation that having the same password for several accounts can raise cybersecurity risks since hackers might exploit credentials that have been compromised to gain access to other accounts. I talked on the significance of making strong, one-of-a-kind passwords, staying away from information that is simple to figure out, and safeguarding key accounts like banking and email. I also discussed the advantages of multi-factor authentication (MFA), such as how extra authentication procedures and verification codes can assist safeguard accounts even in the event that passwords are stolen. I also cautioned against giving out passwords or verification codes.
### Analysis:
By teaching an elderly person about account security and password safety, this program increased cybersecurity awareness. The conversation emphasised the value of robust authentication procedures and showed how user awareness can lower the risk of online fraud and unauthorised account access.
