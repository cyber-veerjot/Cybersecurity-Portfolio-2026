## B14 - Teach your friends about cybersecurity topic of your choice
### Description:
I discussed account security and social engineering attacks with my friend. The objective was to raise awareness of the methods attackers use to force victims to disclose private information, as well as strategies to prevent such attacks.
### Discussion Summary:
Using straightforward language, real-world examples, and helpful guidance, I discussed the subject with my friend.
In the conversation, I talked about:
* What social engineering is: manipulating individuals rather than breaking into systems
* Examples include urgent phishing communications like "your account will be locked."
* How criminals pose as reputable companies
* Important warning indicators (strange sender details, urgent messages, fraudulent links)
* Safe practices include checking messages and avoiding clicking on questionable links.
* The significance of multi-factor authentication (MFA) as an extra security measure
My friend participated in the conversation, asked questions, and recognised the significance of turning on MFA and exercising caution when using the internet.
### Analysis:
This exercise illustrates how human behaviour is frequently cybersecurity's weakest link. The possibility of successful phishing or scam attempts can be decreased by teaching consumers about social engineering practices. This is a successful proactive security strategy since it increases knowledge and promotes safer online behaviours.
