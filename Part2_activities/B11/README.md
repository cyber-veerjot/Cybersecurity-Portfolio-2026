## B11 - Talk to 2 cybersecurity experts from the industry and find out their latest projects
### Expert 1 – Biljana R:
I had a conversation with Biljana R regarding her work in cybersecurity and compliance for critical infrastructure.
### Latest Project:
Her recent work involved conducting vendor and software risk assessments for businesses before new systems are connected to operational environments. This involved examining the locations of critical data storage, system connections, and software interactions with Industrial Control Systems (ICS) and Operational Technology (OT).
### Key Things Learned:
-Critical infrastructure systems prioritise availability because outages can affect public safety.
- OT systems manage physical infrastructure, including industrial processes and power.
- Cybersecurity experts evaluate vendors and products to find security threats prior to implementation.
- Risk evaluations are crucial when managing operational or private data.
### Expert 2 – Caitriona Forde:
Caitriona Forde and I had a conversation regarding organisational security awareness and human issues in cybersecurity.
### Latest Project:
Her recent work focused on improving employee cybersecurity behaviour and reducing human error within organisations. Phishing awareness testing, employee security training, and promoting the use of more robust security measures like password managers and multi-factor authentication (MFA) were all part of this.
### Key Things Learned:
- Many cyber problems are the result of human error and social engineering attacks.
- Organisations use simulated phishing emails to test employee awareness.
- Training on security awareness lowers the risks of credential theft and phishing.
- An essential component of organizational cybersecurity is human behaviour.
### Analysis:
This activity helped me understand real-world cybersecurity projects currently being worked on in industry. I discovered that cybersecurity experts tackle both human-focused security issues like lowering phishing threats and raising employee awareness as well as technological security issues like safeguarding vital infrastructure systems. The discussions showed how modern cybersecurity combines risk management, technical controls, and human behaviour to improve organisational security.
